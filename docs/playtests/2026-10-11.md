# Playtest log, 2026-10-11 (builder): BOT RUNS, NOT A HUMAN PLAYTEST

**Every run in this file is a bot run.** A bot played the game. No person played it. Feel judgments need a human playtest (T5 is still open for that). Bot numbers are not player numbers (D7).

## What was run
- Build: standalone Windows Development build of PulseArenaGame (Unity 6000.4.0f1), Match scene only. The build is in the git-ignored `PulseArenaGame/Builds/StandaloneGc/`. Probe: `docs/evidence/T3/probe/StandaloneGcProbe.cs`.
- Two bots, each playing stages 1 to 5 with seeds 1 to 50 (500 games):
  - **greedy** (careful): prefers a free tile whose symbol is already in the tray, a triple first, then a pair. It avoids starting a new symbol once the tray holds five.
  - **uniform** (careless): taps any free tile at random.
- Pacing: one tap every 0.5 s of game time, the D7 rule. Game time is the match clock (Core), so time to clear is game time.
- Raw runs: `docs/playtests/2026-10-11-bot-runs.csv` (500 rows).

## Confusing moments (observed by the builder, not by a player)
These come from the screenshots and the bot runs. They are not player reactions.
1. The popup "+N" can sit under the bottom "Reduce motion: off" button at 1280 x 720 in landscape (`docs/evidence/T3/popup-over-tray.png`). Readability needs a human look.
2. A win shows "Level cleared" and the Next stage button after the result panel slides in. A loss shows "Tray full" and Retry. Neither is confusing in the bot runs, but both are only visible after the level ends.
3. Lost tray tiles stay red until Retry. A human may read that as a bug or as the loss message.
4. The Reduce Motion button is at the bottom of the screen and its text changes between "off" and "on". A player may not notice that motion can be turned off.

## What this log does and does not change
- D7 says stage sizes stay unchanged until this log exists. The bot win rates sit inside the D7 bands, so this log proposes **no change to stage sizes**.
- The bots approximate the simulator's careful and careless players. They do not prove that a human plays like either one.
- Human playtest still needed for: feel (motion timing, popup), whether covered tiles are understood, the blocked-tap feedback, and the confusing moments above.
