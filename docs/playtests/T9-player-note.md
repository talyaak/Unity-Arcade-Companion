# Pulse Arena: playtest note (T9)

Thank you for playing. We are testing the game, not you, so there are no wrong answers. Pulse Arena is an original tile
game. It has no money, no prizes and no gambling.

## Start the game
1. Open the folder you were given. Keep every file in it together.
2. Double-click `PulseArenaGame.exe`. It runs on Windows. No internet is needed.
3. There is no sound yet. That is on purpose.

## The rules
- Each stage is a pile of tiles. Some tiles sit on top of others.
- **Tap a tile that has nothing on top of it.** It moves into the tray along the bottom.
- When **three tiles of the same shape and colour** are in the tray, they clear, and you score points.
- The tray holds **7 tiles**. If it fills up without a set of three, you lose the stage: "Tray full".
- **Clear every tile on the board** to win the stage: "Level cleared".
- There are five stages. Win one to go to the next.
- Tapping a covered tile does not move it. It flashes red, and it also shakes unless Reduce motion is on.

## Buttons
- **Retry**: appears after a loss. Starts the stage again.
- **Next stage**: appears after a win.
- **Reduce motion**: top right. Turns off the shake, the confetti, the camera nudge and the short pause on clears.
  Moves and colour changes stay. Press it again to turn it back on.

## What we want to know
Please keep a timer running. For each stage, write down:
1. **Time to clear** (or that you lost, and how many tries it took).
2. **Did you understand the rules on stage 1 without reading anything?** If not, what did you try?
3. **Did any tap do something you did not expect?**
4. **Did the motion feel right?** The moves, the shake, the red flash, the "+points" popup and the confetti. Say what
   felt too slow, too fast or too much.
5. **Could you read the score, the tray count and the popup?**
6. **Did you find the Reduce motion button**, and did it change how the game felt?
7. **Anything that broke, froze or confused you.** Give the stage, what you did, and what happened.

## Sending it back
- Write your answers in the order above, with the stage number next to each one.
- Add your Windows version and your screen size.
- A screenshot or a short video helps, if you can take one.
- Please do not share the build outside the playtest group.

Thank you.
