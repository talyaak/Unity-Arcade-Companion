WHAT CHANGED IN THE GAME (Game/Physics/PhysicsMatchController.cs)
- Spawn: each tile starts at a random place over the plate, with a random turn about the vertical axis. Tiles of one wave keep 1.3 units apart centre to centre. A tile's footprint at any turn fits in a circle of radius 0.636, so two tiles in one wave cannot overlap at spawn. A wave that has no room after 60 tries per tile starts a higher wave.
- Waves are 1.0 unit apart, plus up to 0.15 units of random height, so tiles of different waves do not overlap at spawn either.
- Drop: the first wave starts 1.2 units above its resting height (was 0.3).
- The walls rise above the highest spawn (1.5 units of margin).
- Unchanged: which tile goes to which place (shuffled, as before), the free-set rule and thresholds (a 0.36 half width, 0.12 high box above the top face; 0.15 units/s; 1 rad/s; every 3 frames), the look, the materials, Reduce Motion, Core, and D15.
- Harness: Quaternion.Euler added to the compile stubs. GameCompile: 0 errors, 14 warnings, as before.

FREE SET AGAINST THE RAY TESTS (9 runs; checked at the first settle; stage 1 has 12 tiles, stage 2 has 21)
The game's rule is a box: no body within 0.12 units above the top face, within 0.36 units of the centre. Four physical checks were run on each tile:
- Centre ray: one ray up the centre, 1 unit long, from 0.02 above the face. Disagreements: 1 (stage 1), 2 to 3 (stage 2).
- Nine rays across the top face, 0.33 apart, from 0.02 above the face, 0.1 long. Disagreements: 1 (stage 1), 2 (stage 2).
- Nine rays from 0.01 inside the face, 0.13 long. Disagreements: 1 (stage 1), 1 to 2 (stage 2).
- Dense check: 49 rays, 0.12 apart, across the whole box footprint, from 0.01 inside the face, 0.13 long. Disagreements: 0 in all 9 runs.
Reading: the sparse checks sample the box at points 0.12 to 0.33 apart. A neighbour that touches the top face between two sample points, or a tilted corner, is inside the box and is missed by the rays. The mismatch lines in the run files show the dense check agreeing with the box in each case. The dense check was added in this round, after the sparse checks disagreed, so it was designed after seeing the mismatches. The architect should judge whether to accept it as the truth test. The sparse results are kept in the files for transparency.
- Covered tap: Blocked in every run (tile id 0). With Reduce Motion on, the tile moved 0.0000 units. With it off, it moved 0.0003 to 0.0061 units: a blocked tap adds a small torque (a wobble), by design, and the pile moves with it.
