WHAT IS IN THE BUILD
- PhysicsMatchController (Game/Physics): one rigid body per tile. BoxCollider 1 x 0.6 x 1 at scale 0.9; Rigidbody mass 1, linear damping 0.05, angular damping 0.1, interpolate, continuous dynamic. An invisible floor box under the plate (1 unit thick) and four walls keep the pile in. MaxBodies = 60, checked at spawn.
- Free set (the game computes it; Core decides everything else, D14 rule 2). A tile is free when all three hold:
  (a) no other body overlaps a box above its top face: 0.36 units half width, 0.12 units high (RestHalfWidth, RestGap);
  (b) its linear speed is at most 0.15 units/s (SettledSpeed);
  (c) its angular speed is at most 1 rad/s (SettledSpin).
  The set is recomputed every 3 frames from the start of the level and reported to Core (MatchBoard.ReportFreeTiles) when it changes. The level starts with an empty report, so nothing is tappable until the first report. A tile that comes to rest while the pile is still falling can be reported free before the pile has settled.
- Taps go to Core (MatchBoard.TapTile). A covered tap is Blocked: the tile does not move. It shows the shared red flash, plus a small wobble when Reduce Motion is off.
- Tiles keep the T10 look and the shared materials. A covered tile is dimmed by the grid's factor (0.45 luminance).
- HUD: the same UI Toolkit HUD as the grid game, reading IMatchStatus. The grid controller implements the same interface.
- Stages: stage 1 (12 tiles) and stage 2 (21 tiles) were played. Next stage from stage 2 goes to stage 3 (30 tiles); it was run once (see the restart column) and is outside the stage 1 and 2 scope.

FIX MADE IN THIS ROUND
- Retry and Next stage after a clear. The controller kept a reference to every tile body, including the bodies of tiles whose GameObject had been destroyed when their clear finished. ClearTiles then set isKinematic on a destroyed body (MissingReferenceException), so the new stage did not start. The fix removes a body from the controller's list when its view is removed on a clear, and ClearTiles skips destroyed bodies.
- This was found by reading the code after the first run. The earlier probe never restarted, so the failure was not reproduced in a run. The restart runs below were run with the fix and show exceptions=0.

- The score is the same because each win is seven clears in one combo chain. Points per clear are 100 x min(combo, 4), so 100 x (1+2+3+4+4+4+4) = 2200. The chain breaks only when two clears are more than ComboWindowSeconds (3.0 s) apart, and the probe taps every 4 frames, so it never breaks. FINDINGS FOR THE ARCHITECT (Core and generator; not changed here)
1. A loss needs at least four symbols in the level. With three symbols, at most six tiles fit in the tray without a triple, so a tray of seven always forms one first. Stage 1 (three symbols) cannot be lost by the tray rule, in the grid game or in physics mode.
2. The generator draws each triple's symbol independently (MatchLevelGenerator: random.NextInt(spec.SymbolCount) per triple). For stage 2 (four symbols, seven triples), all four symbols appear in 8,400 of 16,384 equally likely draws, about 51 percent. The other layouts have a symbol with no tiles, and a stage with such a layout cannot be lost. Normal play seeds from the clock (Start, Retry, Next stage), so about half of the stage 2 layouts a player can get cannot be lost. Seed 20261011, used for the win runs, is one of them.
3. Decision needed from the rule owner: guarantee that each symbol appears at least once in a level, or accept that three-symbol layouts cannot be lost. Nothing was changed in Core or in the stage specs.
