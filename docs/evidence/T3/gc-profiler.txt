GC Allocated In Frame (ProfilerRecorder, category Memory). This is the counter behind the Profiler's GC Alloc column.
Measured in Editor Play Mode (Unity 6000.4.0f1). A standalone build was not measured. One sample per frame.
Sampling: the sampler reads cached references only and makes no FindAnyObjectByType call per tick. An earlier sampler did, and its numbers were discarded.

IDLE, all match components enabled (no taps; sleep window, no eval calls running): 1,697 frames
  median 9,013 B; p90 9,065 B; min 9,013 B; frames over 50 KB: 49 (2.9%); largest 7,383,974 B

IDLE, every match component disabled (TileView, MatchController, MatchHud, MatchEffects, MatchPopup): 1,821 frames
  median 9,013 B; p90 9,065 B; frames over 50 KB: 41 (2.3%); largest 8,280,798 B

ANIMATION after warm-up (stage 5; 6 taps with moves, a clear, arcs and a popup; sleep window): 321 frames
  median 9,013 B; p90 9,065 B; frames over 50 KB: 3 (tap frames); largest 611,207 B

AFTER the animation (60 frames): median 9,013 B; largest 9,099 B

READING
  The steady 9,013 B per frame is the Editor's play-mode overhead. It is the same with every match component disabled.
  The match components add no measurable steady allocation per frame, in idle or during animation.
  The spikes (about 2 to 3% of frames) also appear with the components disabled. The tap frames are the only ones with extra allocation.
  TARGET NOT MET in the Editor: 0 B per frame would need a standalone build or a device run (T7). Not done here.
