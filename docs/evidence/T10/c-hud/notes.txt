HUD (UI Toolkit, D6; styling in Assets/PulseArena/UI/MatchHud.uss; built in Game/Match/MatchHud.cs):
- Top bar: a stage badge, the score, and the Reduce motion button at the top right (same behaviour as before).
- Progress panel: "Cleared n / total" with a bar (cleared over the level's total), and "Combo n" with a bar (full at 5,
  stays full above 5).
- Tray panel at the bottom: "Tray n / 7" and seven slots. A slot is lit when a tile is in it, and the lit slots turn warm
  (coral) when the tray holds six or seven.
- Result card (Level cleared or Tray full, the score, and Next stage or Retry): slides in over 0.3 s as before. The buttons
  have a darker lower border that shrinks when pressed (:active). The result card is not in these two frames (see Not verified).
- No timers, stars, lives, coins, shop or boosters (D4; no money-like items).

Framing: the camera frames the board in the band between the HUD blocks (HudTopReserve 0.2 and HudBottomReserve 0.1 of
the screen height). The view direction does not change: the camera moves along its own up axis, so the 2.5D angle holds.
- Portrait: the top block ends near 17 percent of the height; the bottom block starts near 93 percent. The board sits
  between them and fills about 84 percent of the width.
- Landscape (1920 x 1080): a compact style (USS .hud-landscape, set on the root when the screen is wider than tall) keeps
  the top block near 25 percent and the bottom block near 8 percent. The first draft overlapped the top stacks and the tray;
  that was fixed in this step, and the second captures show no overlap.

Warm-up: every label and button draws WarmUpText for two frames before the first tap (unchanged rule). WarmUpText now covers
the new labels: letters in both cases, digits, and the words the HUD shows.

Build and checks: Unity recompile 0 errors; GameCompile 0 errors (stubs extended, gate unchanged); Development standalone
build 0 errors (6 warnings, from the temporary probe); player log 0 exception lines.
